How It Works

A complete journey from registration to employment

Every stage of the trainee journey is connected through a single digital workflow.

1

Registration

Trainees, trainers and providers create their accounts.

2

Verification

Authority verifies eligible users and records.

3

Training

Verified trainees are assigned to official batches.

4

Monitoring

Attendance, progress and assessments are tracked.

5

Employment

Employment outcomes are followed after training.

Built for transparent skill development

SkillTrack Maharashtra connects the complete training ecosystem — from verified beneficiaries and training providers to batch management and employment outcomes. The platform creates better visibility for authorities while helping ensure that training reaches the right beneficiaries.

🔐

Verified Records

Only verified trainees, trainers and providers participate in official training.

📊

Data Visibility

📈

Training Tracking

Attendance, progress and assessments are managed throughout the training lifecycle.

💼

Outcome Tracking

Employment follow-ups help measure the real impact of skill development programmes.
